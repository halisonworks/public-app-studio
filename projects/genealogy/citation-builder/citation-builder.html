    About these formats: The three outputs are simplified citation forms inspired by the layered "Evidence Explained" style developed by Elizabeth Shown Mills — a full reference note that moves from the specific item to its container, repository, and how it was accessed; a short note for repeat use; and a reordered source-list entry. House styles vary between societies, journals, and software, so treat these as a clean starting point and adapt punctuation and ordering to whatever standard your project follows. Empty fields are skipped automatically so you never get doubled commas or semicolons.
