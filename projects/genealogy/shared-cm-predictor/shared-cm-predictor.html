About & important caveats

These figures are based on the publicly published Shared cM Project (v4) by Blaine Bettinger, as presented by DNA Painter — the crowd-sourced average and observed ranges of total shared autosomal centimorgans for each relationship.

  • Values are total autosomal cM — a sum of shared DNA, not a percentage.
  • The ranges overlap heavily. A single cM total almost never points to one relationship.
  • cM alone cannot prove a specific relationship. You also need X-DNA, segment counts, family trees, and known relatives to triangulate.
  • Half, “removed,” and multi-generation relationships routinely share the same band, which is why they are grouped together here.
  • This tool is an educational approximation, not professional or legal genealogical advice.
