# DIY / hobby

Practical calculators and planners for everyday making and home projects —
knitting, cooking, gardening, carpentry, and renovation. Each tool is a small,
self-contained app: enter your numbers, get a usable estimate, and keep your
inputs across reloads.

> ⚠️ **Examples, not products.** These tools can contain errors or simplified
> models — they give rough estimates. Verify quantities, costs, and dates
> before relying on them.

Every project ships in **English** (at the project root) and **Norwegian
Bokmål** (a full standalone copy under `i18n/nb/`).

## Projects

- [`knitting-calculator/`](./knitting-calculator/) — **Knitting Calculator**
  (Strikkekalkulator). Convert a pattern to your own gauge, work out cast-on
  stitches and rows for a target size, and estimate how much yarn you need.
- [`recipe-scaler/`](./recipe-scaler/) — **Recipe Scaler** (Oppskrift-skalerer).
  Scale a recipe up or down by servings and convert between common kitchen units
  (g ↔ ml ↔ cups/tbsp/tsp, or dl/ss/ts in Norwegian) using ingredient densities.
- [`planting-calendar/`](./planting-calendar/) — **Planting Calendar**
  (Plantekalender). A month-by-month sow / plant-out / harvest timeline for
  common vegetables, with a climate-zone offset and a bed-spacing planner.
- [`material-calculator/`](./material-calculator/) — **Material Calculator**
  (Materialkalkulator). Estimate paint, flooring / tiles, and lumber for a
  renovation or carpentry job, each with quantity and cost.
