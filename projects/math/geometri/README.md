# Geometri

Fokuserte, interaktive geometriverktøy for matematikk 1P/2P (LK20), på norsk —
hvert verktøy tar for seg ett tema med ulike vinklinger. Til sammen dekker de
geometripensumet på dette nivået.

> ⚠️ Eksempler, ikke ferdige produkter — sjekk utregninger før du stoler på dem.

- [Vinkler](./vinkler/) — Angle types, the angle sum in triangles and polygons, and angle pairs (vertical / supplementary).
- [Pytagoras setning](./pytagoras/) — Pythagoras from several angles: solve a right triangle, a visual area proof, distance between points, and a box space diagonal.
- [Sirkelen](./sirkelen/) — Circle circumference and area, plus sector area and arc length, with an interactive figure.
- [Sammensatte figurer](./sammensatte-figurer/) — Find area and perimeter of composite shapes by splitting them into known parts (or subtracting a cut-out).
- [Volum og overflate](./volum-og-overflate/) — Volume and surface area of prisms, cylinders, spheres, cones and pyramids — with litre conversion.
- [Maalestokk og kart](./maalestokk/) — Map scale both ways (map ↔ real distance), and how area scales with the square of the scale.
- [Formlikhet og kongruens](./formlikhet/) — Similar and congruent figures: scale factor, missing sides, and how area scales with k² and volume with k³.
- [Koordinatgeometri](./koordinatgeometri/) — Distance, midpoint and slope between two points in the coordinate plane.
