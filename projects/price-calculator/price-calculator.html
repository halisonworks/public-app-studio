Key financial point: Discounts cut solely into your profit (the contribution margin). Since the purchase price stays the same, a discount of e.g. 20% will often wipe out all or most of your profit margin.

Theory corner

Markup:

Markup is calculated as a percentage of the purchase price, and shows the amount the business adds to the item before VAT.

Formula: Markup % = (Contribution margin / Purchase price) * 100

Contribution margin ratio (CM%):

The margin ratio shows how much of the selling price excl. VAT is left for fixed costs and profit.

Formula: CM % = (Contribution margin / Price excl. VAT) * 100

How adjustments affect your risk level:

The sales volume is determined directly by your contribution margin in kroner:

  • If you increase the margin, you need to sell fewer items to cover your fixed costs.
  • If you cut prices or give discounts, the required sales volume shoots up to compensate for the lost profit.

Formula: Fixed costs / contribution margin per unit = break-even volume
